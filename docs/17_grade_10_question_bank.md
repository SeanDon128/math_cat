# Grade 10 Question Bank

The Grade 10 static bank contains 570 Algebra I questions with no easy items: 285 medium and 285 hard.

## Standards Coverage

- Linear relationships: equations, multi-step equations, variables on both sides, graphing, slope-intercept form, systems, and inequalities.
- Quadratic relationships: solving by factoring, roots, vertices, and axes of symmetry.
- Algebraic structure: polynomial addition, subtraction, expansion, and factoring with GCF and trinomials.
- Functions: notation, evaluation, tables, and interpretation of linear relationships.
- Applied algebra: age, distance-rate-time, revenue, and population modeling.

## Topic Coverage

Linear Equations (40), Multi-Step Equations (40), Variables on Both Sides (30), Graphing Linear Equations (35), Slope-Intercept Form (30), Systems of Equations (40), Linear Inequalities (35), Solving Quadratic Equations (50), Quadratic Functions (40), Polynomial Operations (35), Factoring Polynomials (50), Function Notation (25), Function Evaluation (25), Function Interpretation (25), Algebra Word Problems (40), and Real-World Modeling (30).

All Grade 10 records set `uses_pi` to `false` because this Algebra I scope contains no circle or geometry questions.
