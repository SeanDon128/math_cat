# Grade 12 Question Bank

The Grade 12 static bank contains 585 advanced high-school mathematics questions: 293 medium and 292 hard. It blends Pre-Calculus, advanced trigonometry, AP Calculus AB foundations, AP Statistics foundations, matrices, and vectors.

## Standards Coverage

- Trigonometric values, radians, unit-circle coordinates, identities, and graph interpretation.
- Limit evaluation, continuity, derivative rules, tangent slopes, rates of change, and AP-style derivative applications.
- Function analysis, compositions, inverses, exponential/logarithmic functions, and sequences and series.
- Probability, sampling, confidence intervals, variability, matrices, vectors, and quantitative modeling.

## Topic Coverage

Advanced Trigonometry (41), Unit Circle (41), Trigonometric Identities (30), Limits (51), Introductory Derivatives (61), Rate of Change (30), Function Analysis (30), Composite Functions (25), Inverse Functions (25), Exponential Functions (25), Logarithmic Functions (25), Sequences and Series (30), Probability (30), Statistical Inference (30), Matrices (25), Vectors (25), Real-World Modeling (25), and AP Calculus-Style Problems (36).
