# Grade 9 Question Bank

The Grade 9 static bank contains 385 questions designed as a bridge from Grade 8 mathematics to Algebra I.

## Standards Coverage

- Hard Grade 8 review: equations, functions, graphing, slope, Pythagorean theorem, systems, exponents, scientific notation, and transformations.
- Algebra I preview: linear equations and graphs, systems, inequalities, quadratic equations and functions, polynomial operations, factoring, and function notation.

## Topic Coverage

Grade 8 review: 130 hard questions. Algebra preview: 89 easy and 166 medium questions. Topic labels carry their curriculum band to distinguish Grade 8 review from Algebra preview topics with the same name.

This requested curriculum contains no circle content, so all records set `uses_pi` to `false`.
