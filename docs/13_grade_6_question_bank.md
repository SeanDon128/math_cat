# Grade 6 Question Bank

The Grade 6 static bank contains 330 multiple-choice questions aligned to the New York State Next Generation Mathematics Learning Standards (2017, updated June 2019).

## Standards Coverage

- NY-5.NBT.7: decimal operations to hundredths
- NY-5.MD.5: volume of rectangular prisms
- NY-5.NF.4 and NY-5.NF.7: fraction multiplication and division foundations
- NY-6.RP.1-3: ratios, unit rates, equivalent ratios, and proportional reasoning
- NY-6.NS.5-7: negative numbers, opposites, comparison, and operations
- NY-6.EE.2 and NY-6.EE.7: evaluating expressions and one-step equations
- NY-6.SP.3 and NY-6.SP.5: measures of center and variation
- NY-6.NS.6 and NY-6.NS.8: coordinate-plane basics
- NY-6.G.1-4: area, perimeter, angle, and line relationships
