- NY-8.F.1-5: functions, input/output relationships, and rate of change
- NY-8.G.1-8: transformations, congruence, Pythagorean theorem, and geometry
- NY-7.RP, NY-7.NS, NY-7.EE, NY-7.G, NY-7.SP: Grade 7 foundation review
