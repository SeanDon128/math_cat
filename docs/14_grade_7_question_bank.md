# Grade 7 Question Bank

The Grade 7 static bank contains 410 multiple-choice questions, aligned to the requested Grade 6 review and New York State Next Generation Mathematics Learning Standards Grade 7 domains.

## Standards Coverage

- NY-7.RP.1-3: ratios, proportional relationships, unit rates, and percent problems
- NY-7.NS.1-3: operations and ordering with rational numbers
- NY-7.EE.1-4: expressions, two-step equations, inequalities, and real-world applications
- NY-7.G.1-6: scale drawings, geometric constructions, circles, angle relationships, area, and volume
- NY-7.SP.5-8: probability, samples, populations, and statistics review
